## One line

A first-person submarine game in a sea grown from any words you type.

## Short description (≈50 words)

Type any phrase and Mnemoquarium grows a sea from it: every word becomes a
species with its own colour, size, temper and price. Dive, net what you find,
sell it at your station, refit the boat, and go deeper. The same words always
grow the same sea — so a sea is something you can share.

## Longer description

Mnemoquarium started as a terminal program: a deterministic artificial-life
tank where each word of a phrase became a species that ate, bred and mutated.
*The Deep* puts you inside it. The same genetics now grow a four-kilometre sea
with a continental shelf, a kelp forest, a twilight drift and an abyss, and
every species in it comes from your words.

You pilot a small submarine out of the Hull, a seabed station. The loop is
simple and old: catch, sell, upgrade, go deeper. Pressure is the gate — the
stock casing is rated to 140 metres, and everything worth real money lives
below that. What lives below that, mostly, is the problem.

When you dock, you climb out and walk the station: the market, the drydock
where each refit is bolted onto your boat where you can see it, and a tank
that keeps two of every species you have ever brought home.

## Features

- Seas grown deterministically from any phrase — share a phrase, share a sea
- A 4 km map from a sunlit shelf to past 1,500 m, with seamounts, trenches,
  wrecks, vent fields and whale falls
- A walkable station with visible upgrades on your boat
- Twelve-plus hostile creatures, from barracuda packs to the Kraken of Static
- Peaceful marine life: octopus, moon jellies, mantas, turtles
- Photo mode with captioned, shareable images; a daily shared sea
- Runs in any modern browser, on phones and with controllers; no install

## For streamers

Every sea comes from words, so a stream can play a sea grown from the
streamer's name, their channel, or whatever chat types. Link format:
`https://sebby1770.github.io/mnemoquarium/?phrase=your+words+here`.
