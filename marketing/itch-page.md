## Description

> **Type any words. Dive the sea they grow.**
>
> Every word you type becomes a species — its colour, its size, its temper,
> its price. The same words always grow the same sea, so you can learn a trench
> the way you learn a room, and send the phrase to a friend so they can dive
> it too.
>
> **The loop.** Leave the Hull, net what lives down there, and bring it home.
> Sell the hold, refit the boat, and go deeper than the stock casing will let
> you. Everything worth real money lives below your rating.
>
> **The station.** Dock and you climb out into the moon pool and walk it: the
> market, the drydock where every refit is bolted onto your boat where you can
> see it, and the ops room, where a tank keeps two of every species you have
> ever caught.
>
> **What lives here.** Octopus that change colour and ink away from you. Moon
> jellies. Mantas. Turtles. And further down: reef sharks, barracuda packs,
> glass squid, lantern anglers, a thing that lies in the silt looking like the
> floor, an octopus the size of a car that wraps itself over your window and
> blacks it out with ink, and the Kraken of Static, which is not a fair fight
> yet.
>
> **Features**
> - A 4 km sea grown from your words, from a sunlit shelf down past 1,500 m
> - Walk your station; watch your refits appear on the boat
> - Photo mode — every shot is captioned with the phrase that grew it
> - Today's Sea: one phrase for everyone, every day
> - Plays in the browser on desktop, phones and tablets, with a controller
>   or with thumbs
> - No install, no account, saves in your browser
>
> Made by one person with three.js and no build step. Source on
> [GitHub](https://github.com/Sebby1770/mnemoquarium).
